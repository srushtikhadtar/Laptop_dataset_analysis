## Business Problem

The laptop market contains products with different brands, prices, specifications, processors, RAM, storage, and operating systems. It can be difficult to compare these products and understand **pricing patterns, popular brands, and customer-oriented specifications**. This project uses SQL to analyze the laptop data and generate useful business insights.

## Objectives

* Analyze laptop brands and their product availability.
* Understand laptop **price ranges and average prices**.
* Compare specifications across different laptop brands.
* Analyze RAM, ROM, processor, GPU, and operating systems.
* Identify highly rated laptops.
* Find patterns between **price and specification ratings**.
* Generate useful insights using SQL queries.

## Key Business Questions

1. Which laptop brands offer both low-priced and high-priced models?

2. Which laptop brands offer the most number of laptops?

3. How many laptops are available for each processor type?

4. Which laptops have both high ratings and low prices?

5. Which laptop brands have an average specification rating above 75?

6. What is the price range of laptops for each brand?

7. Which processors are used in more than 10 laptops?

8. Which laptops have a specification rating above the overall average?

9. Which laptops have a price above the overall average price?

10. Which brands have more than 5 laptops priced above ₹1,00,000?
